FAQ

Straight answers.

How is this different from the free Portfolio Lab?

The Portfolio Lab is free and it's good. It has the full BRRRR course, calculators, a weekly live Q&A with Antonio and me, and around 1,870 investors. It teaches and reviews at scale.

The Inner Circle is one on one. I know your capital, your market, and your deals, and I work through them with you. It's application only, so the time actually goes to you.

You also get access to the Portfolio Lab as part of the Inner Circle, so you keep the course, the calculators, and the weekly live Q&A too.

How is this different from Deal Engine DFY?

With DFY, my team finds, remodels, and rents the property. You own it and you don't do the work. That's the right move if you have capital and no time.

In the Inner Circle, you do the work. You learn to find, underwrite, and run deals yourself, with me advising. DFY client stories like Andy and Nick are DFY results, not Inner Circle results.

If you want DFY later, Inner Circle members get lower rates on it.

Do you buy, rehab, or manage properties for me?

No. That's DFY. Here, you buy, you manage the rehab, and you own the decisions. I help you get them right. If you ever want my team to do it for you, Inner Circle members get lower rates on DFY.

Do I get deals or Deal Room access?

You get access to the Portfolio Lab, and the Deal Room lives there. The Inner Circle itself is about underwriting and running the deals you bring.

I haven't bought a rental yet. Can I still apply?

Yes. First rental with no experience is something I teach all the time. What matters is that you're ready to act. The application asks about your capital and timeline so we can be honest about fit.

How often do we talk, and what does it cost?

Calls run [CALL_CADENCE], with messaging between calls ([MESSAGING_SLA]). The program runs for [PROGRAM_DURATION]. There's no set price to buy online. Apply, and if it's a fit we go over everything on the call.

I don't invest in Oklahoma. Does that matter?

I operate in Lawton, Oklahoma. The underwriting, rehab, and refi logic travels. Your market's prices, rents, and rules are yours to know, and we'll talk through how to check them.

Is this financial advice?

No. This is mentorship and consulting from an operator. It is not financial, legal, tax, lending, or securities advice. Talk to licensed pros for those.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Real estate puts capital at risk. My portfolio numbers are mine, not yours. What we guarantee is the process. We keep working your underwriting and your plan on the calls until it's decision ready.
